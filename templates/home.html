Welcome to Forum CIO

Forum CIO is a platform built for the UVA community to discover, review, and rate Contracted Independent Organizations. Browse CIOs across grounds, read honest reviews from fellow students, and find the right organizations for you. Whether you are looking for career development, community involvement, or new experiences, Forum CIO helps you make informed decisions.

Sign in with your UVA account to leave reviews, create CIOs, rate organizations, and message other students. You can also sign in as a guest to browse and explore CIOs.
